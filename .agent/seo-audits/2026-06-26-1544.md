## Executive Summary

**Overall health: A- / Excellent foundation.** The site was built with SEO best-practices baked into the generator (`seo-src/template.mjs` + `generate.mjs`), with hard validation gates on title/meta length, required fields, section counts, and FAQ counts. Traditional technical + on-page SEO is essentially clean — there are **0 critical and 0 high-severity technical/on-page issues**.

The remaining opportunity is almost entirely in **AI / answer-engine discovery (AEO/GEO)** and a few **schema + coverage** enrichments. Since the site has near-zero live traffic today (pre-launch waitlist — see Data below), the work targets *future* discovery rather than reacting to current behavior.

### Top opportunities (highest leverage first)
1. **No `llms.txt` / `llms-full.txt`** — the site is invisible to the emerging LLM-discovery convention. (AI-SEO, P1)
2. **`robots.txt` doesn't explicitly welcome AI crawlers** — works via wildcard, but no explicit signal or `llms.txt` pointer. (AI-SEO, P1)
3. **Schema can be enriched** — `HowTo` on how-to guides, `DefinedTerm` on glossary terms → richer Google results + far stronger AI citability. (Schema, P2)
4. **Coverage gaps** — a handful of high-intent comparison/how-to queries have no page yet. (Programmatic-SEO, P3)
