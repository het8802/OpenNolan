## Top 10

### Motion

| Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `transition: all 0.12s` — `.q-option` (styles.css:119) | `transition: background-color 120ms ease, border-color 120ms ease` | `all` transitions every future property you add, including layout ones; name what moves. |
| `transition: all 0.12s` — `.option-btn` (styles.css:199) | `transition: background-color 120ms ease` | Same; the only thing that actually changes on hover is the background. |

| Zero `:active` states in 1341 lines (styles.css) | `transform: scale(0.97)` on `.st-btn`, `.st-ico`, `.st-play`, `.st-seg`, `.st-chip`, `.tile`, `button`, `.art-chip`, `.fb-dir` | The rubric's first component principle. Nothing in the app confirms it heard a press. |

| `scrollIntoView({ behavior: 'smooth' })` on every stream chunk (ChatPanel.jsx:38) | `behavior: busy ? 'instant' : 'smooth'` | Overlapping smooth scrolls cancel each other; the transcript judders for the whole turn. |
| `{!playing && …}` mounts/unmounts the 64px `.st-stage-play` with no transition (StudioPreview.jsx:501, styles.css:940) | Keep it mounted; `opacity`/`scale(0.96)` crossfade, 160ms `ease-out` | A 64px element popping in and out of the dead center of the preview on every play/pause. | `.st-notice` / `.st-banner` inserted into flow, no transition (Studio.jsx:675, :681) | Absolute-position over `.st-bar`, or reserve height and crossfade | Shifts the preview canvas *and* the timeline on every save. See Top 10 #7. |

| **Chat panel** (`ChatPanel.jsx`) | **4/10** | Interleaved text/tool ordering, stick-to-bottom-only-when-parked, and the API-key card are all right. But the smooth-scroll-per-chunk judder runs through every turn, the render bar is fabricated, the Send button stretches to 220px, and the empty state — the single most important onboarding surface for the ICP — is one line of 3.62:1 grey. |
| **Timeline** (`StudioTimeline.jsx`) | **4/10** | Ambitious and mostly correct: sticky lane gutter, derived placement, one clean pointer model, per-lane eye toggles with real ARIA. But the playhead animates `left` every frame, clips are entirely keyboard-inaccessible, trim handles are invisible until hover, delete has no undo affordance, no drag surface shows `grabbing`, and the toolbar ships a color emoji next to two fullwidth CJK glyphs. |
| **Dashboard** (`App.jsx` `Dashboard`) | **3/10** | The layout, tile clamping and hover lift are fine; the copy is clear. But random-hue rainbow gradients on every tile actively fight the brand on the app's landing screen, three identical secondary pills compete in the header, "BYOK" is jargon, and loading is indistinguishable from empty. |
| **Pipeline / Activity panel** (`App.jsx` `WorkPanel`) | **3/10** | Genuinely useful information architecture — the stepper, "How this was made", and op badges are good ideas. Execution is the weakest in the app: `{}` as an icon, an emoji hourglass, `transition: all` plus a redundant infinite pulse on every running row, mouse-only file rows, and an ambiguous Raw/Formatted toggle. |
| **Design system** (`styles.css` as an artifact) | **2/10** | It is a stylesheet, not a system. Sixteen color tokens and one radius token sit on top of 35 font sizes, 13 radii, 10 shadows, 60 hardcoded hex values, 6 disabled opacities, 0 spacing steps, 0 motion tokens, 2 focus states, 0 press states, and 23 `filter: none` overrides undoing its own global button rule. Every other score on this table is capped by this one. |
