# Video Craft Learning Loop

Use this for Marketing OS video production and for recurring improvement jobs.

## Core correction

A short-form video must feel edited, not assembled. Do not treat a video as a sequence of generated images. A production draft should include real motion: licensed B-roll clips, screen recordings, UI captures, generated video, animated diagrams, moving overlays, SFX hits, and intentional cuts.

## Research before production

Before making a new recurring format, study current platform patterns from YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, creator breakdowns, editing tutorials, and competitor accounts. Capture:

- Hook type: curiosity gap, contrarian claim, urgent opportunity, social proof, mistake/avoidance, before/after, open loop.
- First 3 seconds: visual event, text promise, voice tone, pattern interrupt.
- Cut rhythm: average shot length, where captions appear, when zooms/transitions/SFX happen.
- Retention devices: open loops, progress markers, quick payoffs, contrast, stakes, novelty, human face/reaction, status/identity triggers.
- Motion language: camera movement, parallax, kinetic typography, screen-recording zooms, overlays, cursor movement, callouts.
- Audio language: music bed, whooshes, impacts, risers, silence drops, voice cadence.
- CTA style: explicit action, search phrase, save/share trigger, comment prompt.

Save learnings under `~/marketing-os/video-craft/YYYY-MM-DD.md` and update this skill when a recurring rule emerges.

## Production standard

For each script:

1. Beat-map the script by seconds, not paragraphs.
2. Assign each beat a moving visual source: licensed B-roll, screen recording, UI animation, generated video, or motion graphic.
3. Use still images only as supporting inserts; animate with layers/internal motion, not simple slideshow pan/zoom.
4. Add a retention event every 2–4 seconds: cut, zoom, overlay, prop/UI change, caption pop, SFX hit, speed ramp, transition, or framing shift.
5. Make captions selective and punchy. Do not paste the whole narration on screen.
6. Mix audio intentionally: voice, quiet bed, SFX accents, pauses.
7. QA with full playback plus contact sheet. Contact sheet alone cannot prove the video feels like video.

## QA checklist

- Does at least 50–70% of runtime contain actual moving footage, screen recording, UI animation, generated video, or meaningful internal motion?
- Are there cuts or retention events every 2–4 seconds?
- Are there at least 3 types of motion, e.g. footage + UI animation + overlays?
- Does the first 3 seconds have a visual pattern interrupt?
- Are captions readable, sparse, and synced to emphasis?
- Does the final export decode and have no black frames?

## Failure signals

- Looks like generated posters stitched together.
- Same camera move repeated across scenes.
- Captions carry the whole video while visuals are decorative.
- Contact sheet looks varied but playback feels static.
- No real footage, screen recording, product interaction, or moving UI.
