## Durable rule for future morning scripts
When creating or revising your morning scripts, do not settle for the first hook. Run a 5-angle hook rewrite pass:

1. Bold claim
2. Personal confession / experiment
3. Surprising stat or quantified shift
4. Direct question
5. “You’ve been doing X wrong” correction

Then choose the version with the strongest founder/operator tension and immediately prove it in the next line.
