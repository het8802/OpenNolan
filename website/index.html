    Why your videos aren't landing

    Great product. And nobody's watching.

    Short-form is how products get discovered now — but the videos that actually travel don't look like the ones founders make.

    The first second decides it

    Scrollers bail in under a second. With no hook to stop the thumb, your view count dies before your pitch even starts.

    Flat and slow gets skipped

    Calmly talking to camera is a desktop habit. The feed rewards fast cuts, motion, memes and pattern interrupts — editing you don't have time for.

    Editing eats your week

    Hunting trending audio, cutting B-roll, syncing captions, adding proof — it's a part-time job you never signed up for.

    The short-form playbook

    Vertical video is a fight for attention — and it has rules.

    Every reel that travels runs the same formula. OpenNolan bakes it in: hand it your clips and the agent finds the trending pieces and assembles the high-paced, vertical cut for you — so you ship videos that follow the playbook without studying it, or editing it.

    01

    Hook in <1s

    Open on the payoff or a pattern interrupt before anyone can scroll.

    02

    Cut every 1–3s

    Hard cuts and motion reset attention and keep the thumb still.

    03

    Trending sound

    Ride the audio and memes the algorithm is already pushing.

    04

    Captions on mute

    85% watch with sound off — burned-in captions carry the message.

    05

    Show receipts

    Screenshots, stats and proof make the claim land and convert.

    How it works

    From product to posted — without the editing grind.

    1

    Drop in your clips

    Hand OpenNolan your raw footage — screen recordings, talking-head, B-roll — or just describe the video. Add your own API key, no account, no subscription.

    2

    The agent makes the whole reel

    It pulls supporting images and B-roll from the web, adds sound effects and music, and cuts everything into rapid, motion-driven sequences tuned for vertical — no editing from you.

    3

    Post it — or tweak first

    It's done. Export vertical for Reels, TikTok and Shorts — or fine-tune any cut, caption or clip on a real timeline first. Every edit is instant.
