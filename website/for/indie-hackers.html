Turn your ship log into shorts without the editing tax

Your changelog is already content. OpenNolan just turns it into shorts.

Every push, every demo, every screen recording is raw material. The problem has never been having something to show — it's that turning a 40-second screen grab into something the feed actually pushes takes hours you'd rather spend building.

OpenNolan flips the order. Drop in your clips (or just describe the video), and the AI agent does the editor's job: trending hooks and fast cuts, explainer animations, screenshot proof with auto-highlight, B-roll and images pulled from the web, music and sound effects, captions burned in for the 85% who watch on mute. You get a finished vertical cut tuned for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts — built on the actual short-form playbook, not guesswork.

Bring your own key: cost control that fits a bootstrapped budget

Most editing tools want a monthly subscription whether you ship one video that month or thirty. That math doesn't work when you're funding everything yourself.

OpenNolan is bring-your-own-key (BYOK). You add your own AI provider API key and pay providers directly — no markup, no subscription, no account required. You're in full control of spend: a quiet month costs you almost nothing, a launch week scales with what you actually use. The model is local-first too, so your clips and files stay on your Mac instead of someone else's cloud.

And because it's open source, there's no vendor lock-in to worry about. You can read the code, see exactly what it does with your files and keys, and trust it the way an indie hacker trusts any tool in their stack.

  • Pay AI providers directly — no markup, no subscription
  • No account required; spend scales with your actual usage
  • Local-first: your files stay on your Mac

Render once, edit cheap — keep your shipping cadence

Ship a short between commits, not instead of them.

Speed is the whole point. The agent gives you a finished cut, but you stay the editor-in-chief. Post it as-is, or tweak first on a real timeline: reorder, retrim, retime, swap a caption, move an overlay.

OpenNolan renders once, then most edits preview instantly without re-rendering. Only AI-composition scenes re-render, and only the one scene you changed. So a quick fix before you post takes seconds, not a fresh export — which is exactly how a solo maker keeps a daily or weekly #buildinpublic rhythm without the timeline becoming a second job.

Frequently asked questions

Is OpenNolan available to download yet?

Not yet — OpenNolan is a Mac app in active development, and this site is the waitlist. Join it and you'll be first to know the moment it ships. No spam, just the launch. If you build in public, getting in early means you're ready to turn your next milestone into a short on day one.

How does pricing work for a bootstrapped maker?

There's no subscription and no markup. OpenNolan is bring-your-own-key: you add your own AI provider API key and pay those providers directly for what you use. No account is required. A slow month costs you almost nothing; a launch week scales with your actual usage — cost control that fits a solo budget.

Is it really open source?

Yes. OpenNolan is open source, so you can read the code, see exactly what it does with your clips and API keys, and trust it the way you trust the rest of your stack. There's no vendor lock-in, and because it's local-first, your files stay on your Mac rather than on someone else's servers.

Can I turn screen recordings and ship logs into shorts?

That's the core use case. Drop in screen recordings, demos, or clips and the AI agent builds a vertical cut — adding hooks, fast cuts, captions, music, and screenshot proof with auto-highlight. It's tuned for product demos, so your changelog and #buildinpublic updates become shorts without you learning a timeline.

Does it run on Windows or in the browser?

Not right now. OpenNolan is a native macOS desktop app — not a web app and not Windows. That's a deliberate choice for a local-first, render-once model that keeps your files private and your edits instant. If you're on a Mac, join the waitlist and you'll be first in line when it ships.
