## What OpenNolan is (one sentence)

OpenNolan is a **Mac app** where an **AI agent turns your raw clips into a finished,
scroll-stopping vertical video** (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) — and you
can fine-tune any cut on a real timeline afterward.

## Product facts (accurate — safe to state)

- **Platform:** native **macOS desktop app** (not web, not mobile, not Windows).
- **Model:** a **hybrid of AI-agent editing + human editing**. The agent starts the
  project, builds the composition, places clips, adds music/SFX, pulls B-roll and
  images from the web, writes captions, animates overlays, and assembles a vertical
  cut. The human reviewer then edits clips, cuts, captions on a real NLE timeline.
- **Vertical-first:** built for **9:16 / 1080×1920** short-form. Exports for Reels,
  TikTok, Shorts.
- **"Render once, edit cheap":** local-first rendering. After the first render, most
  edits (reorder, retrim, retime, transitions, reposition, text) preview instantly
  **without re-rendering**. Only AI-composition clips (Remotion/HyperFrames) re-render,
  and only the one changed scene.
- **Bring your own key (BYOK):** you add your own AI provider API key and pay providers
  **directly — no markup, no subscription**. No account required.
- **Local-first / private:** runs on your Mac, your files stay on your machine.
- **Open source.**
- **Capabilities the agent can do today:** trending hooks + fast cuts, explainer
  animations (Remotion / HyperFrames), screenshot proof with auto-highlight,
  AI-generated B-roll video, avatar / talking-head with background removal, music +
  sound effects (ElevenLabs), keyframe animations (position/scale/opacity), and a real
  timeline editor (cuts, overlays, text, images, audio, transitions, crop, speed).
- **In progress / honest caveats:** trending stickers/GIFs search is a **representative
  mock** in the demo, not shipped. The app itself is **not released yet** — this site is
  a **waitlist**.

## The short-form playbook (durable facts we teach)

- **Hook in under 1 second** — open on the payoff or a pattern interrupt.
- **Cut every 1–3 seconds** — hard cuts and motion reset attention.
- **Ride trending sound** — the algorithm already pushes it.
- **Captions on mute** — ~85% watch with sound off; burned-in captions carry the message.
- **Show receipts** — screenshots, stats, proof make the claim land and convert.
